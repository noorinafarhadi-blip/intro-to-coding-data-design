Afghan cuisine is full of delicious flavours and traditional recipes.Here are three of my favourites.

Qabul

i Palao

A famous Afghan rice dish made with rice, meat, carrots, raisins and delicious spices.

Cooking: 60 minutes

Afghan Mantu

Delicious steamed dumplings filled with seasoned minced meat and served with youghurt and tomato sauce.

Preparation: 40 minutes

Cooking: 40 minutes

Afghan Bolani

A traditional Afghan Flatbread filled with potatoes, spring onions or vegetables.

Preparation:30 minutes

Cooking:20 minutes
